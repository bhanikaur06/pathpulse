🧭 Pattern learning

After a few trips on the same route, Path Pulse recognizes it as normal — and only raises a flag when you genuinely go off-script.

🧠 ML anomaly scoring

An Isolation Forest model trained on real GPS trajectories scores every ping on location, time of day, and speed — not just distance.

🚨 Escalating SOS

Unconfirmed unusual trips trigger a visible countdown, then an automatic alert to your emergency contacts — plus a silent secret code word and voice trigger for when you can't reach your phone.
